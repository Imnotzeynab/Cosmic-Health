HEALTH INDICATOR 06

SLEEP HEALTH

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

This indicator helps you monitor your sleep, rest, and sleep-wake patterns.

During long-duration space missions, disrupted routines, altered light-dark cycles, workload, isolation, and the mission environment can affect sleep and recovery.

You can use this check-in to identify changes in sleep duration, sleep quality, nighttime awakenings, daytime sleepiness, or other sleep-related changes.
