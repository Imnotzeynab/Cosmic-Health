HEALTH INDICATOR 04

BREATHING & LUNGS

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

This indicator helps you monitor your breathing and overall respiratory function.

During long-duration space missions, changes in the space environment can affect breathing, lung function, and respiratory health.

You can use this check-in to identify shortness of breath, coughing, chest tightness, breathing changes, or other respiratory symptoms.
