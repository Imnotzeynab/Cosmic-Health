COSMIC HEALTH

Understanding Your Health
During Long-Duration Missions

NASA HEALTH is a health-monitoring concept designed to help astronauts track changes in their physical and behavioral well-being during long-duration space missions.

OUR MISSION

Long-duration space missions place unique demands on the human body and mind. Changes in sleep, movement, cardiovascular function, respiratory health, mental well-being and other health indicators can affect an astronaut's ability to carry out a mission.

NASA HEALTH provides a structured way to record symptoms, monitor their severity and observe changes over time.

HOW IT WORKS

01

CHECK IN

Select the health areas and symptoms you are experiencing.

02

DESCRIBE

Provide information about what you are experiencing and how long it has been happening.

03

RATE

Indicate the severity of each selected symptom.

04

MONITOR

Review your results and track changes in your health history over time.

HEALTH HISTORY

Each completed health analysis can be recorded as a daily health snapshot. These records allow changes in reported symptom severity to be viewed over time.
