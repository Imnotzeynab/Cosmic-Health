HEALTH INDICATOR 02

CARDIOVASCULAR

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

This indicator helps you monitor the health of your heart and circulatory system.

During long-duration space missions, microgravity and fluid shifts can affect cardiovascular function and blood pressure.

You can use this check-in to identify changes in heart rate, heart rhythm, blood pressure, circulation, exercise capacity, or other cardiovascular symptoms.
