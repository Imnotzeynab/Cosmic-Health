HEALTH INDICATOR 03

IMMUNE & GENERAL

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

This indicator helps you monitor your immune system and overall physical health.

During long-duration space missions, isolation, stress, radiation, and changes in the space environment can affect immune function.

You can use this check-in to identify signs of illness, infection, inflammation, unusual fatigue, fever, or other changes in your general health.
