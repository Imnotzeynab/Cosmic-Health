HEALTH INDICATOR 01

MUSCULOSKELETAL

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

This indicator helps you monitor the health of your muscles, bones, and joints.

During long-duration space missions, changes in the space environment can affect the musculoskeletal system.

You can use this check-in to identify pain, discomfort, stiffness, weakness, or other symptoms.
